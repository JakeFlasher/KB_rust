## What the rule says

The Inland Revenue Department publishes the maximum tax rates that a jurisdiction with a Comprehensive Double Taxation Agreement/Arrangement with Hong Kong may charge a Hong Kong resident on dividends, interest, royalties and technical fees. For the Chinese Mainland (effective year of assessment 2007/2008), the dividend columns are 5% for qualifying companies and 10% otherwise. The accompanying notes define a qualifying company by reference to Article 10 of the relevant agreement — for the Mainland, a 5% rate where the beneficial owner holds directly at least 25% of the payer's capital.

## Why it matters for an option-income writer

For a Hong Kong investor holding Mainland-incorporated shares (e.g. H-shares), the CDTA caps the Mainland dividend withholding that erodes the dividend leg of a buy-write or dividend-capture strategy. The cap (5%/10%) is the institutional fact behind why net dividend yield on Mainland names differs from the headline figure; figures are as of the snapshot date below.

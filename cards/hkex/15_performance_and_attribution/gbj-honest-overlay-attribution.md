## Thesis

He runs option-selling (sell-put / sell-call) only as a small, fully-collateralized experimental sleeve and is explicit that it is a high-risk game he does not recommend to ordinary investors: never sell-call without holding 100% of the underlying, and never sell-put without the full cash ready to take delivery ('會死人㗎！'); he also rules out leverage outright ('用融資是找死', '不用杠桿是底線'). On that footing he measures the sleeve's TRUE contribution against the whole book, not the deployed slice: ~19% book gain on the ~10% of capital deployed over 3+ months is only ~1.9% to the total portfolio. He stresses the result is path- and regime-dependent — it may be owed to the main underlyings (阿里巴巴/腾讯/中国太保) drifting mildly up over those months ('這可能與...走勢溫和向上有關') — and that for a name like Tencent (700) the sell-put outcome and simply holding the shares would diverge a lot, so he keeps the role modest and treats it as an ongoing experiment ('實驗繼續').

## The arithmetic: measure the sleeve against the whole book

In late June 2022 he posted a deliberately deflating tally of his option-selling experiment (the figures are a snapshot of his 2022-H1 record, not a durable return claim): 「三月上旬sell put至今，三個多月，只計算占用10%的資金，sell put sell call帶來的賬面收益19%，約為股票組合創收1.9%。」 — "From early March until now, three-plus months: counting only the ~10% of capital occupied, sell put and sell call produced a 19% book gain — roughly 1.9% of income for the stock portfolio." The honest denominator is the whole portfolio, not the deployed slice: a striking-looking 19% shrinks to about 1.9% once attributed to total capital, which is why he keeps the sleeve's role modest.

In the same breath he qualifies the result as regime-dependent: 「這可能與sell put主力對象$阿里巴巴-SW(09988)$ $腾讯控股(00700)$ $中国太保(02601)$ 這幾個月走勢溫和向上有關，實驗繼續…」 — "this may be related to the main sell-put underlyings — Alibaba, Tencent, China Pacific Insurance — drifting mildly upward over these months; the experiment continues..." He does not claim the income is skill; he flags that the favorable path of the underlyings may explain it. The evidence base should be read honestly, too: these two quotes are consecutive sentences of a single post (which he also reposted verbatim as a comment under his earlier risk-disclaimer thread), and the exchange below is one follow-up reply — a single self-audit plus one elaboration, not a long-running attribution series.

## One underlying is not another: the 700 caveat

When a commenter observed that simply holding Alibaba through the same stretch would have matched the option result without any derivative, he agreed the comparison is unsettled and kept it specific: 「所以還要實驗，如果是700，sell put和持有實股就就差很遠了。」 — "That's exactly why the experiment must continue; if it were 700 (Tencent), sell-put and holding the actual shares would differ a great deal." He assigns no direction to that gap — only that for Tencent the two paths diverge widely, so a three-month sample on mildly rising underlyings settles little. 實驗繼續 — "the experiment continues" — is his operative stance, not a victory lap.

## The footing: fully collateralized, no leverage, not for ordinary investors

The sleeve rests on a hard risk floor he published as an explicit disclaimer so beginners would not imitate him: 「本人一向反對普通投資者觸碰任何的衍生工具，即便是sell call和sell put」 — he has always opposed ordinary investors touching any derivative, even sell call and sell put — because it is a high-risk game: 「手中沒有100%的正股，千萬不要sell call；同理，沒有準備好全額接貨的錢，不要sell put。會死人㗎！」 — never sell a call without 100% of the underlying shares in hand; likewise, never sell a put without the full cash ready to take delivery. "It can kill!" He sells puts only against cash he actually holds — 「我sell put有多少錢就接多少的貨，絕不多賣」 ("I sell puts only for as much stock as my cash can take delivery of, never more") — rejects margin financing outright (「用融資是找死」, "using financing is courting death"), and draws the boundary plainly: 「不用杠桿是底線，忌貪！」 — no leverage is the bottom line; guard against greed. The ~1.9%-of-book attribution is only meaningful on this fully collateralized, unlevered footing: the deployed 10% is real cash and real shares, not levered notional exposure.

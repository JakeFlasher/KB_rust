## Thesis

Over many years the author has held that the single most important factor is the holding mentality (持股心态) — especially at bottoms, where watching prices keep falling erodes confidence until you end up reducing (减仓) exactly when you should be adding (加仓). Because of that failure mode, he concludes ("所以均衡的组合也很重要") that a balanced portfolio is also very important — i.e., balance is motivated as a guard against bottom-of-cycle capitulation, though he does not elaborate the mechanism further: the "without capitulating on any one name" elaboration and the "behavioral backstop vs. risk metric" contrast are interpretive extensions, not his words. This is a single, once-stated reflection (recurrence 1) in a deliberate top-level post, phrased in the author's long-standing-conviction language ("这么多年，我一直觉得...最重要").

## The failure mode, in his own words

In a deliberate top-level post (June 2022, tagging 中国太保, 联邦制药, and 中国移动 amid that year's drawdown) he wrote: 「这么多年，我一直觉得持股心态最重要……尤其是底部，很容易跌着跌着信心都跌没了。结果是该加仓的时候跑了去减仓」 — "All these years, I've always felt the holding mentality matters most... especially at the bottom, where it is all too easy for confidence to fall away as prices keep falling. The result: exactly when you should be adding (加仓), you run off and reduce (减仓) instead." Two things carry the weight here. The framing 这么多年，我一直觉得...最重要 ("for all these years I've always felt ... matters most") marks this as long-standing conviction rather than a mood of the moment. And the failure mode is described mechanically, not as panic in the abstract: confidence depletes step by step as price falls, so the action actually taken at the bottom (减仓) is the precise inverse of the action the moment calls for (加仓).

## Balance as the drawn conclusion — and where he stops

He immediately draws one structural conclusion: 「所以均衡的组合也很重要」 — "so a balanced portfolio is also very important." The connective 所以 ("therefore") ties portfolio balance causally to the capitulation failure mode just described — balance is offered as a guard against it, not as an unrelated aside. But that sentence is the full extent of his claim. He does not spell out how balance protects the holding mentality, and he states the principle exactly once in the 2022-H1 corpus; nearby thread replies are stock-specific fundamentals, not restatements. Read the card at exactly that strength: a once-stated, long-conviction reflection with the causal arrow present (所以) and the mechanism left unelaborated.

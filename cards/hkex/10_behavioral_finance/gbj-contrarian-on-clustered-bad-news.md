## Thesis

A sector-wide sentiment trough from clustered bad news (profit warning + lockdown + accident) can be the entry opportunity for a cyclical name ('share hardship' rather than avoid). He reads HK 'poor-May, dismal-June' (五穷六绝) seasonality against the dividend calendar — many ex-div names only pay cash in July, so don't over-fear it — and uses index relative-strength (HSI vs Dow decoupling) as a bottoming tell.

## Sharing hardship at the trough

On 18 June 2022 he floated Shanghai Petrochemical's H shares as a reader-directed, conditional suggestion — 如果你喜歡化工行業，為什麼不考慮$上海石化(SH600688)$ H？ ("if you like the chemicals sector, why not consider Shanghai Petrochemical H?") — precisely because the bad news had piled up: 一季度盈警+上海信封+大爆炸，是一個很好的機會與之共患難 — "a Q1 profit warning + the Shanghai 信封 + the big explosion is a very good opportunity to share hardship (共患難) with it." The reading discipline has three parts. First, the simultaneous bundle of negatives is itself the signal: when everything bad lands on a cyclical at once, sentiment is at its trough. Second, 共患難 frames the entry as deliberately sharing the company's adversity rather than waiting for the news to clear — by the time the news clears, so does the price. Third, the framing is "if you like the sector, why not consider" — an offer to a reader, not a prediction or a general rule. One gloss to keep marked as interpretive: 上海信封 is literally "Shanghai envelope," an oblique phrase; reading it as the Shanghai lockdown is an interpretation (a contemporary commenter was equally puzzled: 信封？ — "envelope?"). The thread also pushed back in real time — one commenter retorted 这叫抱薪救火，何来患难与共 ("that's carrying firewood to fight the fire — what 'sharing hardship'?") — and he let the suggestion stand.

## 五窮六絕 read against the dividend calendar

Three days earlier (15 June 2022) he defused the HK adage 五窮六絕 ("poor in May, dismal in June") with a mechanical cash-flow observation: 五六月除淨的港股，很多都是七月份才派股息，不應太悲觀 — "many HK stocks that go ex-dividend in May–June only pay the dividend in July, so one shouldn't be too pessimistic (不應太悲觀)." Part of the seasonal weakness is just ex-dividend price drops landing while the cash has not yet reached investors' accounts; the July payment wave is a scheduled tailwind. He returned to exactly this theme on 2 July 2022: 五窮六絕都熬過去了，翻身月份就別太擔心了，大家收到股息又開始手癢了 — "we've endured through 五窮六絕; don't worry too much in the turnaround month — everyone has received their dividends and their hands are itching again," i.e. the paid-out dividends become redeployable buying power chasing the same market.

## The HSI-vs-Dow decoupling tell

In the same 15 June post he stated his bottoming tell: 如果今天$恒生指数(HKHSI)$ 再不跟$道琼斯指数(.DJI)$，大概可以判断港股已經跌不下去了，五窮六絕到此結束 — "if today the Hang Seng Index again fails to follow the Dow Jones down, one can roughly judge (大概可以判断) that HK stocks can't fall any further, and 五窮六絕 ends here." The mechanism is relative strength: when the local index stops tracking the leading overseas index lower, the marginal seller is exhausted. His hedging is in the wording itself — 大概可以判断 is "can roughly judge," a probabilistic read, not a call. When a commenter argued the turn had come much earlier, his only reply was 我315那天喊見底！ ("I called the bottom on March 15!"), defending a prior bottom call rather than upgrading this read into a forecast. One synthesis note for the reader: the clustered-bad-news entry (18 June) and the seasonality/relative-strength read (15 June) come from two separate posts on different dates — each is independently sourced, but he never presented them together as a single combined method.

## Dated State

Every strike, premium and price level in this card comes from the author's 2022-H1 Xueqiu corpus snapshot (a handful of utterances run to 2026-03) and none of it is a durable recommendation. The dated levels: CNOOC (00883) July-2022 sell put at 10 collecting 0.48, July-2022 sell call at 10.5 collecting 0.47, an earlier CNOOC sell call at 12 and sell put at 10, and his then-current judgment that roughly 8.5 was very hard for CNOOC to break.

## Thesis

He sells cash-secured puts and covered calls to harvest option premium (volatility/time-value) rather than to bet on direction; the ideal outcome is a range-bound, flat underlying ('不死不活'), and his edge comes from respecting probabilities anchored to a fundamental floor (e.g. judging CNOOC's ~8.5 hard to break given mid-cycle oil earnings and dividends), not from a directional forecast. CRITICAL author caveats that must travel with this principle: he is on record opposing ordinary investors using any derivatives at all, and insists you must NOT sell a call unless you hold 100% of the underlying, and must NOT sell a put unless you have the full cash ready to take delivery ('會死人㗎' — you can get wiped out). He sells only what he is willing to be assigned on and never oversells, and treats leverage as an absolute red line ('用融資是找死'; '不用杠桿是底線，忌貪').

## The ideal outcome is a stock that goes nowhere (不死不活)

Reflecting on his July-2022 Tencent and Alibaba puts, he stated the seller's payoff in one line: 「sell put最希望股價不死不話」 — a put seller most wants the share price "neither dead nor alive" (his 不死不話 is a slip for the idiom 不死不活, which he uses elsewhere), pinned in a range while time-value decays in his favor. Asked in the same thread whether he was still going after Tencent, he answered 「不死不活最符合我的利益」 — "neither dead nor alive serves my interest best." He ran the principle as a concrete pair on CNOOC: 「我sell 了$中国海洋石油(00883)$ 七月份10元的put，收0.48；又sell了$中国海洋石油(00883)$ 七月份的call，收0.47元。你說不死不活，我是不是應該很開心」 — "I sold the CNOOC July 10 put for 0.48, and also sold a CNOOC July call for 0.47. You say it's neither dead nor alive — shouldn't I be very happy?" (the call strike was 10.5, per his same-thread disclosure). He was answering commenter 郭荆璞's 不死不活 description of the stock, and he never labeled the put-plus-call pair a short strangle; reading it as one is an inference from the strikes, not his own framing. What he himself emphasized is the payoff logic: with both legs sold against full collateral, a stock that stays pinned between the strikes through expiry leaves him roughly 0.95 per share of premium on a 10-level underlying in about a month.

## No forecast — a floor-anchored probability judgment

When a reader asked whether the CNOOC call meant he was predicting the price would stay below the strike, he rejected the framing: 「和我之前sell call12元和sell put 10元一樣，不預判股價。」 — "Same as my earlier sell call at 12 and sell put at 10: I do not predict the share price." The rest of the same post supplies what replaces the forecast: even at mid-cycle oil prices CNOOC earns richly and pays a fat dividend, so in his judgment ~8.5 was very hard to break; selling the 10 put for 0.48 meant bearing a month-plus of risk with 「概率上對我有利」 — the probabilities on his side. On the call leg he admitted reluctance to part with shares, 「但我尊重概率」 — "but I respect the probabilities." The strike is anchored to a valuation floor; the premium is compensation for variance around that floor. He claims no skill in calling direction — only the discipline of selling strikes the fundamental floor makes improbable to breach.

## The risk spine: eligibility, full collateral, and the leverage red line

These caveats are not an afterthought — they are the body of the very post the 不死不活 line sits under. He is on record opposing ordinary investors touching any derivatives at all, these two trades included. His hard rules: without 100%的正股 (100% of the underlying shares) never sell a call; without 全額接貨的錢 (the full cash to take delivery) never sell a put — 「會死人㗎」, you can get wiped out. Sizing follows the same rule: 「sell put有多少錢就接多少的貨，絕不多賣」 — sell only as many puts as you have cash to be assigned on, never oversell; when sizing the CNOOC put he counted the dividend landing before expiry as part of that cash (「至少知道會有股息馬上到賬，這是原則」 — knowing the dividend cash arrives immediately is a principle). Financing assignment on margin is, in his words, 「用融資是找死」 — using borrowed money is courting death — and 「不用杠桿是底線，忌貪」: no leverage is the bottom line, beware greed. One honest nuance: he once described a call written against a pending put assignment (shares contracted but not yet delivered) as 半裸 ("half-naked") — a tightly hedged personal exception he tolerated for himself, not part of his public prescription, which is fully collateralized on both legs. Operationally the fully collateralized call leg is also capital-light on HKEX: a writer who owns the underlying may use the shares "to replace cash for the margin of writing the call," so the base position simultaneously covers delivery and satisfies margin.

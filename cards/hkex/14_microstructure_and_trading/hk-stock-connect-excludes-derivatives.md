## What the rule says

HKEX's Stock Connect FAQ states that among SSE-/SZSE-listed securities, only A shares and ETFs are included in Shanghai and Shenzhen Connect; other product types such as B shares, bonds and other securities are not included. Stock Connect also encompasses only secondary-market trading, not primary-market activity such as IPOs.

## Why it matters for an option-income writer

A Hong Kong-based investor running an option-income strategy cannot reach Mainland single-stock options or other derivatives through Stock Connect — the Northbound channel admits only cash A shares and ETFs. Option strategies on Chinese companies are therefore practical only on the Hong Kong-listed universe (e.g. H-shares and HK-listed names with listed options), not on Mainland A-share lines via Connect.

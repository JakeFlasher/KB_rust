## What the source says

The Investor and Financial Education Council advises that an investor who prefers physical share certificates should make sure the certificates are registered in the investor's own name, so that the share registrar can send dividends, shareholder circulars and other corporate documents directly to the holder. Registration in another party's name redirects those entitlements.

## Why it matters

The contrast with nominee (CCASS) holding is the practical point: only the name on the register receives dividends and corporate communications directly. For an investor weighing paper versus electronic holding of dividend-paying HK shares, registration determines whether dividends and notices arrive directly or via an intermediary chain.

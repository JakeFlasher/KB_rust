## Dated State

The levels in this card are from the author's 2022-H1 corpus snapshot (a handful of utterances in the corpus run to 2026-03) and are NOT durable recommendations. The dated levels: for 联邦制药 (03933), a 2022 first-half EPS floor of RMB 0.45 ($0.45人民幣) and a 2022 full-year EPS floor of 0.8元 — the full-year figure was not currency-labeled by the author; an RMB reading is implied only by the questioner's framing. Both were stated in June 2022.

## Thesis

He underwrites on a probability-weighted, downside-anchored basis: set a satisfactory base-case EPS rather than chasing optimistic targets, and treat 50%+ growth off a non-small prior-year base as greedy. He bases the buy decision on confidence in restored shareholder returns (payout ratio), not just the operational-recovery story, and refuses precise full-year calls when policy (集采) drives genuine uncertainty — giving a floor EPS instead of a point forecast.

## Satisfactory floor, not stretch target: 联邦制药 (June 2022)

After attending the 联邦制药 (03933) shareholder meeting, he posted a writeup covering 6-APA capacity utilization in the 80-90% range, an insulin price cut of roughly 50% under 集采 (centralized procurement, with the full-year picture still hanging on second-half bids from peers), a fast-growing veterinary-drug line, and the company's first-ever special dividend. When a commenter asked whether first-half net profit could really grow 50%+, he answered:

> 去年上半年基數并不小，希望增長50%以上有點貪心了。如果今年上半年每股盈利能夠有$0.45人民幣，我已經非常滿意了。

"Last year's first half was not a small base — hoping for 50%+ growth is a bit greedy (貪心). If first-half EPS this year can reach RMB 0.45, I am already very satisfied." Two mechanical moves sit in one sentence: check the prior-year base before extrapolating a growth rate (a non-small base makes a 50%+ hope greed, not analysis), and define the EPS level at which you are satisfied — a base case you are content to own through, not an optimistic target that must come true.

When the thread pressed for a full-year number (a commenter asked whether the full year would reach RMB 1 per share), he declined the point call and quoted only a floor:

> 6Apa和胰島素集采都有不確定性，所以全年業績我也不好判斷，0.8元應該會有

"Both 6-APA and insulin 集采 carry uncertainty, so I cannot really judge the full-year result; 0.8元 should be there." Where a policy variable — here, centralized-procurement pricing rounds still in progress — genuinely drives the outcome, he does not manufacture a precise forecast: he names the uncertainty, refuses the call, and states only the floor he is willing to stand behind. (He attaches no currency to the 0.8元; RMB is implied by the question he was answering, not asserted by him.)

## The payout-confidence screen: 鲁泰 (a different stock, and a no-buy)

In a separate June 2022 exchange about textile maker 鲁泰, he explained why a genuine operational-recovery story did not get him in:

> 魯泰的看點是產能利用率的提升，不過由於我對它是否能夠恢復過去的派息比率不具信心，所以沒有介入。

"魯泰's attraction is the improvement in capacity utilization, but because I am not confident it can restore its past payout ratio, I did not get involved." Read this as recorded: a payout-confidence screen applied as his stated reason for NOT buying this one stock — not an affirmed universal entry rule. The operational story was real (he added that its Vietnam plant sits next to a friend's factory and had plenty of orders that year) and he still passed, because the leg of the return that actually reaches the shareholder — a restored dividend payout ratio — was the leg he could not get confident about.

Across the corpus this discipline appears as exactly three distinct author utterances on these two stocks. The general framing in the thesis is a fair but lightly-supported abstraction from those two concrete instances, and the instances should be kept distinct: 联邦制药 carries the EPS-floor mechanics under 集采 uncertainty; 鲁泰 carries the payout-ratio screen.

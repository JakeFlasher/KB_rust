## Thesis
He avoids buying companies with declining revenue (especially declining sales volume) as likely competitive losers; reads diversification into unrelated ventures ('不务正业') as the core business failing; treats youth disengagement as a leading indicator of ad-revenue erosion regardless of currently stable financials ('得年轻人得天下'); and decodes vague ESG/'green transformation' rhetoric as masking a real underlying problem.

## Revenue Decline and 不務正業 Side-Bets
When a reader remembered his rule as the stricter "never hold a stock with regressing earnings," he corrected it downward: 「我只說盡量避免買營收下滑的股票，尤其是銷售量也是下滑的企業，背後往往代表着那間企業是行業競爭下的輸家。」 — "I only said to try as much as possible to avoid buying stocks with declining revenue, especially companies whose sales volume is also declining; behind that, it often means the company is the loser in industry competition." Two hedges are part of the rule, not noise: 盡量避免 (try to avoid — a tilt, not a ban) and 往往 (often, not always). Falling volume is the sharper tell than falling revenue alone, because volume strips out price effects and shows customers actually walking away; the thread he was correcting concerned cyclically lower oil prices, exactly the kind of revenue dip the soft wording leaves room for.

The second signal reads management behavior: 「一家公司開始不務正業，往往是因為主業真的不行了」 — "when a company starts straying from its proper business, it is often because the core business genuinely no longer works." His worked example was Meta: the VR-headset pivot — 「VR頭盔是什麼鬼？」 ("what on earth is a VR headset?") — read as management's own confession about Facebook, while TikTok's rise eroded user stickiness and time spent, and through them ad pricing and ad revenue. Again 往往: an unrelated side-bet is a strong hint about the core franchise, not proof.

## Youth Disengagement and ESG Boilerplate
When another reader objected that Meta's reported numbers were still stable, he answered with media history rather than financials: 「廣告行業，得年輕人得天下」 — "in the advertising industry, whoever wins the young wins the world." Newspapers faded, ATV is gone, and 電視廣播 TVB (00511) management also once believed its position unshakeable; in his reading, young users had already left Facebook (at most they watch a little YouTube), so the ad-revenue erosion was in motion before the income statement caught up — he expected the regression to show within a few quarters. Commenters pushed back hard on the rule (arguing audience commercial value, not youth, sets ad pricing); he made no concession, so the heuristic stands as his stated view.

The fourth signal appears as a single worked illustration rather than an explicitly stated screen — the reader infers the rule. Asked why 北控水務 (Beijing Enterprises Water) kept falling, he pasted the company's own green/low-carbon/ESG transformation boilerplate and replied only 「你琢磨一下下面那段說話就明白了」 — "mull over that passage below and you'll understand": when a company's story for itself is a wall of transformation buzzwords, treat the rhetoric as covering a real underlying problem rather than describing a plan.

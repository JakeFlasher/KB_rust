# Standard VII Responsibilities as a CFA Institute Member or CFA Candidate

## Intuition

Standard VII.A REQUIRES Members and Candidates to not engage in any
conduct that compromises the reputation or integrity of CFA
Institute or the CFA designation or the integrity, validity, or
security of CFA Institute programs. The Standard reaches conduct
in CFA Institute examinations (exam secrecy, no sharing of
specific questions), conduct as a candidate (responses to CFA
Institute inquiries, accuracy of candidate-status claims), and
conduct that affects the public perception of CFA Institute or
the charter (e.g., public statements purporting to represent CFA
Institute without authority). **Source:** CFA Institute (2022) L1
Vol.6/pp.461-468.

Standard VII.B REQUIRES Members and Candidates, when referring to
CFA Institute, CFA Institute membership, the CFA designation, or
candidacy in the CFA Program, to not misrepresent or exaggerate
the meaning or implications of membership, the designation, or
candidacy. The Standard tightly governs how charter-holders may
describe themselves, how candidates may describe their candidate
status, and the prohibition on implying ongoing study or
recurring-credential renewals where none exist. **Source:** CFA
Institute (2022) L1 Vol.6/pp.469-490.

## Definition

Standard VII.A REQUIRES Members and Candidates to not engage in any
conduct that compromises the reputation or integrity of CFA
Institute or the CFA designation, or the integrity, validity, or
security of CFA Institute programs. The conduct space includes
exam-related conduct (preserving exam secrecy; not discussing
specific exam questions or content publicly post-exam; cooperating
with proctors; not bringing/removing prohibited materials),
candidate-status conduct (truthfully responding to CFA Institute
inquiries; accurately representing candidacy), and
public-representation conduct (not purporting to speak on behalf
of CFA Institute without authorization). **Source:** CFA Institute
(2022) L1 Vol.6/pp.461-468.

The Standard's exam-secrecy provision is narrowly construed: a
candidate may discuss general topic areas covered (the topic
weights are publicly disclosed) but may not discuss specific
question contents, answer choices, or the like. Post-exam
disclosures of specific questions — whether verbatim or
paraphrased, whether to other candidates or in published commentary
— violate VII.A. **Source:** CFA Institute (2022) L1
Vol.6/pp.463-468.

Standard VII.B REQUIRES Members and Candidates, when referring to
CFA Institute, CFA Institute membership, the CFA designation, or
candidacy in the CFA Program, to: (1) not misrepresent or
exaggerate the meaning or implications of membership, the
designation, or candidacy; (2) use the marks (CFA, CFA charter,
Chartered Financial Analyst) according to CFA Institute's published
guidelines. **Source:** CFA Institute (2022) L1 Vol.6/pp.469-490.

The Recommended Procedures for VII.B include adhering to CFA
Institute's published trademark and reference guidelines, ensuring
that any reference to the CFA charter accurately reflects active
membership (membership lapses if dues are unpaid), and using only
the standard formats "John Smith, CFA" or "John Smith is a CFA
charter-holder" for charter-holder reference. **Source:** CFA
Institute (2022) L1 Vol.6/pp.469-490.

## Mathematical Reasoning

The VII.A program-integrity obligation (source REQUIRES) frames the
relationship between the Member or Candidate and CFA Institute as
mutual: CFA Institute confers the designation and grants candidacy;
the Member/Candidate commits to preserving the integrity of the
program. The Standards' enforcement rationale is that the
designation's public-trust signal depends on the program's
integrity; conduct that erodes integrity (exam cheating, false
candidacy claims, public misrepresentation of CFA Institute
positions) erodes the signal for every Member. The Standard is
therefore structurally analogous to a profession-wide externality
internalization mechanism: individual conduct gets policed because
the externality reaches the entire charter-holder population.
**Source:** CFA Institute (2022) L1 Vol.6/pp.461-468.

The VII.B reference-rule discipline (source REQUIRES) operates a
narrow language gate: only the specific forms CFA Institute
publishes are acceptable; any embellishment, abbreviation, or
implied equivalence is a violation. The Recommended Procedures
RECOMMENDS that firms train new charter-holders on the published
guidelines before they update their professional materials (CV,
LinkedIn, business cards); the failure to train results in
inadvertent violations through enthusiastic but non-conforming
self-description (e.g., "Senior CFA Charter-holder" is a violation
even if the holder is in fact senior; the term "Senior" is not a
CFA-Institute-recognized prefix). **Source:** CFA Institute (2022)
L1 Vol.6/pp.469-485.

The candidate-status reference rule (source REQUIRES) is the most
exam-tested VII.B predicate: a Candidate may reference their
candidacy ONLY when they are currently enrolled AND registered for
the next applicable exam administration; a Candidate who passed
Level I but has not yet registered for Level II may NOT reference
"CFA Level II candidate" because the registration is what
establishes the active candidacy. The reference must include both
the level and the session (e.g., "registered for the May 2026
Level II exam"). Once chartered, the Candidate becomes a
charter-holder and uses the charter-holder reference forms.
**Source:** CFA Institute (2022) L1 Vol.6/pp.485-490.

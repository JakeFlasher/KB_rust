Eliminates BCF (Beneficial Conversion Feature) and CCF (Cash Conversion Feature) separation models. Post-2022, US issuers account for most convertible debt as a single debt unit (no equity split, opposite of IAS 32), unless it (a) requires derivative bifurcation under ASC 815 or (b) was issued at substantial premium.
